Privacy Policy

Welcome to Obsidian Project. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, use, and protection.

1. Information We Don't Collect

Obsidian Project is designed with privacy as a core principle. Our platform:

  • Does not collect or store personal information about our users
  • Does not track your activities outside our application
  • Does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • Does not share any user data with third parties

2. Private by Design

Obsidian Project is built to be private and secure. All information within the app is:

  • Private and inaccessible to other users without explicit permission
  • Protected with industry-standard security measures
  • Not used for advertising, marketing, or analytics purposes

3. Limited Data Usage

Any limited information we may process is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain our service
  • Improve and develop our platform
  • Protect the security and integrity of our service
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. User Control

As a user of Obsidian Project, you maintain full control over your data. You can:

  • Access your information at any time
  • Delete your information permanently
  • Update or correct your information

5. Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the security of any information we process. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date at the top of this page.
